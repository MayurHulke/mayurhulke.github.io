# Mayur Hulke - homepage design specification

## Brand promise

Mayur builds dependable physical intelligence for machines operating in the real world. The presentation should feel technically credible, personally motivated and quietly ambitious.

## Protected identity and contracts

- Hero statement: “Building the physical-intelligence layer for robots.”
- Narrative arc: childhood robotics → Chisel → production perception → industrial manipulation → Earth as the proving ground → infrastructure beyond Earth.
- Real factual evidence, existing routes, links, photographs and the detailed `/experience/` page.
- The public homepage remains unchanged until the Orbital Memory preview is explicitly approved for replacement.
- Conference photographs are visual material only; do not add visible captions, claims or a new conference narrative.
- NASA imagery is clearly identified as external research reference material, never as Mayur's work.

## Orbital Memory visual system

- Mode: redesign overhaul, approved after the two-chapter v0.
- World: one persistent photographic orbital memory atlas, controlled by native reversible scroll.
- Palette: void `#030508`, star `#F4F1E8`, lunar dust `#B9B5AA`, Earth ocean `#2C7DA0`, ember `#FF8A3D`, Mars oxide `#B44C35`.
- Typography: local Syne variable font for display and local IBM Plex Sans for reading.
- Material language: lunar mineral terrain, real photographic planes, restrained metal rings and planetary atmosphere.
- Motion: orbital glide, descent, close pass, lateral inspection, atmospheric surface and departure. Reduced motion becomes the complete semantic photographic story.
- Exclusions: neon cyberpunk, HUD chrome, rounded-card grids, emoji as decoration, false affiliations and science-fiction claims presented as current work.

## Interaction principles

- Navigation communicates exact progress and never hijacks the wheel.
- Scroll owns the global journey; pointer movement provides only restrained parallax.
- Every chapter must have a distinct landmark, camera endpoint and story change.
- The same scroll position must restore the same chapter forward, backward and after a fast jump.
- The complete story remains readable when WebGL or motion is unavailable.
